How to link

Takes about 30 seconds.

  1. Open WhatsApp ➜ Settings ➜ Linked devices ➜ Link a device
  2. For a pairing code choose Link with phone number instead
  3. Type the pairing code shown above, or choose Link a device and scan the QR code
  4. Send .menu to your own chat. Your bot answers!

Log in to your bot

No account needed. Your bot sends you the password on WhatsApp.

  1. Open your own WhatsApp chat (message yourself)
  2. Send .webpass
  3. Enter your number and the password the bot replies with
